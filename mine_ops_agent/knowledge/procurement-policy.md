# POL-PRC-002: Maintenance Procurement and Approval Limits

## Who can raise requisitions
Maintenance planners and supervisors can raise purchase requisitions. Technicians can view stock but cannot raise requisitions.

## Approval limits
A requisition up to 5,000 SAR can be submitted by a planner. Any requisition above 5,000 SAR requires supervisor approval before it is submitted to the supplier.

## Reorder rule
When a planned service would take on-hand quantity below the reorder point, raise a requisition that covers any shortfall and restores stock to the reorder point once the service is done.
