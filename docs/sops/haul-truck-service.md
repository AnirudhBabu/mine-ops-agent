# SOP-MNT-014: Haul Truck Preventive Service

Applies to all 240t haul trucks.

## Service interval
A haul truck is due for preventive service every 500 operating hours since its last service. A truck more than 10 percent past its interval (over 550 hours since service) is overdue and must be scheduled within 24 hours.

## Service kit
Each service uses two engine oil filters, one air filter element and one 205L drum of 15W-40 engine oil. Confirm kit availability in stores before releasing the work order.

## Lockout and isolation
Before any service, park on level ground, apply the park brake, chock the wheels and apply personal locks to the isolation point. Never work under a raised body without the body prop pin installed.
