# SOP-MNT-031: Gyratory Crusher Liner Inspection and Change

## Inspection
Inspect mantle and concave liners every 1000 operating hours, or immediately when throughput drops or the crusher is flagged DEGRADED.

## Replacement trigger
Replace the mantle liner when remaining thickness is below 40 mm. A crusher liner change is a planned shutdown and requires supervisor sign-off.

## Spares
Keep at least one mantle liner on hand at plant stores. Mantle liners have a long lead time, so a stock-out must be raised as a high-priority requisition.
